Research/Evolving Worlds
Measurement Validity

Evaluating AI in Evolving Worlds

I study how controlled, evolving environments can reveal whether AI systems reason, adapt, and manage consequences across unfamiliar situations—rather than merely complete isolated tasks.

From Isolated Success to Sustained Competence

A correct answer to a predefined task provides limited evidence about how an agent will behave as information, objectives, other actors, and operating conditions change.

FIXED-TASK EVALUATION

One task, one response, one score

EVOLVING-WORLD EVALUATION

Decisions unfold through interaction

Observe→Decide→Consequence
↺ The world changes and produces new information

A General Evaluation Method

The goal is not simply to generate more scenarios. It is to construct worlds whose controlled mechanisms make behavioral evidence interpretable.

01

Capability claim

Define the competence the evaluation should establish.

02

Required mechanisms

Identify the interactions and dynamics needed to reveal it.

03

Controlled world

Configure actors, information, goals, and constraints.

04

Behavioral trajectory

Observe decisions, updates, consequences, and recovery.

05

Capability evidence

Test counterfactuals and transfer across held-out worlds.

Three Motivating Worlds

The domains differ, but each uses controlled variation to distinguish genuine adaptation from success based on familiar associations.

SOCIAL REASONING

Simulated Courtroom

An agent evaluates evidence while characters exchange incomplete or unreliable information.

VaryBeliefs, demographics, relationships, testimony, and information access.

MeasureEvidence use, belief updating, bias, and counterfactual consistency.

FINANCIAL REASONING

Simulated Economy

An agent forecasts and makes decisions in markets whose familiar relationships may no longer hold.

VaryCompany identities, fundamentals, investor behavior, and news quality.

MeasureForecasting, calibration, uncertainty, and adaptation.

ADAPTIVE AUTOMATION

Simulated Infrastructure

An agent maintains a system while workloads, requirements, failures, and threats evolve.

VaryUsers, services, resources, failures, workloads, and security conditions.

MeasureReliability, intervention quality, recovery, efficiency, and restraint.
